# TR-C extension — Learning airline route preferences from flight-plan revisions

Journal extension of `paper/sid2026` for Transportation Research Part C.
`OUTLINE.md` is the plan; `trc2026.tex` the manuscript; `SUBMISSION_NOTES.md`
the evidence map and the list of things a human must verify before
submission. Build with `~/.local/bin/tectonic -X compile trc2026.tex`.

## Structure decisions (settled, do not re-churn)

- **Class**: `elsarticle` preprint, 12pt, author-year (`elsarticle-harv`),
  single column. Compiles under tectonic with zero undefined references.
- **The SID prose is the core.** Sections that existed at SID were carried
  over nearly verbatim (they are already in the author's voice) and the
  journal sections were written around them: discrete-choice bridge
  (S2.2), literature threads (S3), archive composition + restored maps
  (S4.2), leakage as its own section (S5), heuristic baselines (S7.2),
  strata (S7.3), operating-points table + concentration + no-stay
  sensitivity (S8), horizon experiment (S9), threats to validity (S10).
- **Neutrality**: no operator, waypoint, volume or city pair is named in
  any table. The per-city-pair and per-waypoint tables from the study
  were deliberately left out of the paper. The intro keeps the SID
  Amsterdam–Barcelona anecdote (already published).
- **Figures** are reused from `paper/sid2026/figures/` (copied, not
  symlinked) plus `fig_horizon.pdf` generated by
  `make_horizon_figure.py`. The SID print-at-size rule applies: figures
  drawn at the size they are printed, no scaling at include time.
  Included widths (0.6–0.85 of a ~406 pt text block) are close enough to
  the SID column sizes that fonts remain in the 7.5–9.5 pt band; redraw
  at exact size before submission if a figure looks off at final size.

## The horizon experiment (S9)

`studies/2026-08-trc-horizon/` — production configuration trained on
2025-01→10, tuned + calibrated on 2025-11/12, evaluated month by month
on 2026-01→06 (splits 692,754 / 96,325 / 370,496 pairs). Answers the
retraining-cadence question the main split cannot. Note the premise
correction against `OUTLINE.md`: the fuel study's main split was
already strictly out-of-time, so this run is a horizon study, not a fix
of the audit's month-of-year criticism (which applied to the older
production `skyrank train` SplitConfig, not to the study lineage).
Heavy artifacts in `/data/rdcodina/skyrank/trc_horizon/`.

## Voice pipeline

scientific-writer (structure, evidence binding) → humanizer audit →
the author's voice profile, applied last. Automated checks enforced:
zero em dashes, contractions, And/But/So openers, American spellings,
AI-vocabulary; every section opens with a lead-in paragraph before its
first subsection; abstract under ~250 words.
